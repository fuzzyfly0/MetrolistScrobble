## Fixes

- **Bluetooth car stereos:** Metrolist Scrobble now shows up in the Bluetooth media menu of cars without Android Auto, next to apps like VLC or Transistor, and can be browsed and played from there. Android only offers an app to the car when it can also open audio files, which Metrolist could not do before; opening an audio file with Metrolist Scrobble now simply opens the app
- **Starting from the car:** when the car connects while the app is in the background, the playback service no longer shuts itself down right away

If Metrolist Scrobble does not appear in the car yet, turn Bluetooth on the phone off and on once.

Install over 0.2.1 to keep your settings and playlist.
