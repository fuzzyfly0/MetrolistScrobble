## Fixes

- **End point B:** when a song reaches B, the next song now loads and starts reliably instead of sometimes getting stuck
- **No empty notification:** when a car stereo, Android Auto or another app connects to Metrolist Scrobble without anything playing, the empty placeholder notification disappears again after a moment
- **Android Auto buttons:** the extra buttons (like, shuffle, repeat, start and end point) now show up right away instead of only after the next song change

Install over 0.3.0 to keep your settings and playlist.
