## First release

Metrolist fork that records what you play in the official YouTube Music app and adds it to a local Metrolist playlist automatically.

- Only counts songs you actually listened to (half the song, at most 30 seconds); skipped songs are ignored
- No duplicates: a song that is already in the playlist (same video ID, or same title and artist) is not added again
- No Google login and no API key needed
- Playlist: *Played in YouTube Music* / *Gehört in YouTube Music*
- Runs as a separate app (*Metrolist Scrobble*) next to the official Metrolist

## Setup

*Settings → Integrations → YouTube Music recorder*: turn it on and grant notification access.
