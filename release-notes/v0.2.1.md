## Fixes

- **Songs heard again move up:** when a song that is already in the playlist is played again for at least the set listen time, it is moved to the newest position, as if it had been recorded for the first time. The playlist order now always shows what you played most recently, and the maximum size removes the songs you have not heard the longest
- **German names:** with the app in German, the integration is called *Hörverlauf anderer Apps* and the playlist *In anderen Apps gehört* again. An existing playlist with a default name follows the app language automatically; a name you chose yourself is kept
- **Last activity** shows when a song was heard again and moved

Install over 0.2.0 to keep your settings and playlist.
